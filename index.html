Unbreakable

Spirit.

[PLACEHOLDER THESIS: This interactive digital exhibition explores the multifaceted nature of resistance utilized by enslaved African-Americans, proving that survival itself was a revolutionary act against an institution designed to break human dignity.]

[PLACEHOLDER INTRO: History often distills resistance into a handful of armed rebellions. However, the true story of resilience is found in the daily, dangerous, and brilliantly covert actions taken by millions to maintain their humanity, culture, and minds.]

Intellectual Defiance

[PLACEHOLDER: Literacy was illegal. Learning to read and write was a crime punishable by severe mutilation. Discuss how enslaved individuals stole knowledge, forged passes, and shared news of abolitionist movements, weaponizing information.]

The Hush Harbors

[PLACEHOLDER: Slavers used twisted versions of Christianity to demand obedience. In response, enslaved people created 'Hush Harbors'—secret meetings deep in the woods where they practiced their own faith, combining African traditions with the story of Exodus, singing spirituals that contained coded instructions for the Underground Railroad.]
